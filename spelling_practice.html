Spelling Practice Helper

Use this page to help your 10-year-old practice spelling. Try each activity and check off the words as you master them!

Word List

Fun Practice Ideas

Look, Say, Cover, Write, Check

Read each word aloud, cover it with your hand, write it from memory, and then check your spelling. Repeat until you get each word right twice.

Flashcard Challenge

Make flashcards with the words on one side and clues or sentences on the other. Shuffle them, read the clue, and spell the word aloud.

Story Time

Write a short story that uses every word in the list. Draw pictures to match each part of the story for extra fun.

Mini Spelling Bee

Take turns pronouncing a word while your child spells it out loud. Keep score and offer a high-five or sticker for each perfect round!

Notes & Achievements

Keep track of tricky words, bonus challenges, or proud moments from today's practice.
